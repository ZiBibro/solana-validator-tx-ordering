Method
------

Per burst, with itb.analyze (arrival_ms, slot2_ms, timing_in_band) and the burst_end row:

  dev       arrival estimate minus the band centre (slot2: observed slot 1 to slot 2 step;
            pre300: -300 ms). Band: slot2 -100..+100, pre300 -200..+100 around the centre.
  resid     prediction_residual: node-time first shred of slot 1 minus the final prediction.
  step_err  slot2 only: aimed step (send_target_arrival - send_predicted_first_slot) minus
            the observed step from slot 1 to slot 2.
  late      first send minus the aimed send moment
            (first_shred_node[0] + first_send_node_offset - (send_target_arrival - send_rtt/2)).
  gap       first_slot - send_anchor_slot: slots the final aim extrapolated over.
  ws_lag    receipt minus node time of the first shred of slot 1 (slot stream delivery lag).

dev = step_err - resid + late holds within 0.1 ms on all 116 bursts.

Findings
--------

1. Stale anchor behind the WebSocket backlog. The gap follows the delivery lag of the slot
   stream: Pearson 0.945 between gap and ws_lag over 116 bursts. When events arrive seconds
   late, the last received slot is tens of slots old, and the aim extrapolates over them.

     gap        bursts  misses  median |resid|
     <= 4         71       5        21 ms
     5..16        27       5        32 ms
     > 16         18       8       125 ms

2. The lower share of 28.10 comes from that backlog. Bursts with gap > 16: 5 of 68 in the
   pilot, 13 of 48 in the four test runs (two-sided Fisher exact p 0.0077). Within gap <= 16
   the band share is 58 of 63 against 30 of 35 (p 0.32); within gap > 16 it is 4 of 5
   against 6 of 13. Overall 62 of 68 against 36 of 48 (p 0.021). Median ws_lag per test run:
   932, 2251, 139 and 885 ms; pilot 258 ms.

3. Distant leaders. With a fresh anchor (gap <= 4) the residual has a median of +69 ms for
   leaders with RTT above 100 ms (n 16) and 0.0 ms for the rest (n 55). 5Cchr1 (AgaveBam,
   RTT 162-165 ms) shows 74-113 ms in 7 bursts, 6 of them at 101 ms or more; DRpbCB
   (AgaveBam, RTT 112-115 ms) shows -18 to 76 ms in 7. The slot clock is fitted on the first
   shreds of all leaders as the RPC node receives them, and the shreds of a distant leader
   reach that node later. In the slot2 band (+-100 ms) this alone puts the burst at the low
   edge: misses 93 and 150 (dev -110 and -106). The pre300 band absorbs it.
   Campaign plan campaign-mainnet-e1071-20261028T034518Z (index 0) has 9 bursts of 5Cchr1,
   4 of them slot2, and 9 of DRpbCB, 5 of them slot2.

4. Model step past the step horizon. For slot2 with gap > 16, Model::predict uses the mean
   slot duration for slot 1 and for slot 2, so the aimed step is 212.8 ms at the median (n 10)
   while the observed step inside a window is about 149 ms: step_err +61.7 ms at the median.
   With gap <= 16 the per-position steps give step_err -1.5 ms (n 52). Taking step_err out
   of those 10 bursts brings no miss into the band and moves one burst out (011115Z seq 132,
   dev -81.6 to -142.6): 97 of 116 instead of 98. The defect is real; fixing it alone does
   not raise the band share on these data.

5. Late sends are rare and mostly in the pilot. late is 0.0 ms at the median and 0.1 ms at
   p90 over the 116 bursts. It is above 1 ms in 10 bursts: nine of the pilot on ef77ca3,
   before the stepped sleep of 73f3d20 (54, 60, 71, 72, 76, 77, 97, 100, 103; 11.3 to
   327.6 ms), and one of 28.10 (014101Z seq 140, 51.7 ms, in band). It decides two misses,
   71 (327.6 ms) and 76 (68.2 ms).

6. Not separable from stored data: residuals of 179-277 ms at gaps 5-14 (79, 133, 138, 163)
   and 138 ms at gap 4 with a near leader (88). burst_end keeps the first shreds of slots 1
   and 2 only, so a late window start at the leader handoff cannot be told apart from drift
   over the extrapolated slots. Burst 156 had an observed slot step of 211 ms against the
   aimed 150 ms (step_err -61) on top of a residual of 49 ms.
